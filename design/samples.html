suite design sheet

Every shared piece of the suite, drawn from tokens.css and recipes.css. A pane copies the rules it needs (with node design/embed.mjs) and renames the ui- prefix to its own. Light theme only. Read DESIGN.md for the rules and the pane contract.

Colours

Tokens --sui-*. Primary blue for the header and the one main action; light neutrals everywhere else.

Type, spacing, radii

System font, 15 px body, a 4 px grid, 44 px targets. ui-title 24 · ui-heading 20 · ui-subheading 17 · body 15 · ui-small 13.

Title, 24 px

Heading, 20 px

Sub-heading, 17 px

Body text, 15 px, line height 1.5. Bold is 600; no italics or capitals for emphasis.

Small and muted, 13 px: sublines, captions, dates.

Header bar

ui-header, ui-header-btn, ui-header-apps (current: aria-current="page"), ui-header-app, ui-header-spacer, ui-header-search, avatar. 50 px tall, primary blue, white.

ui-popover ui-launcher › ui-caption, ui-launcher-grid › ui-launcher-tile › ui-launcher-badge (ui-alt for tool-index entries). Menus: ui-popover › ui-menu-head, ui-menu › ui-menu-item.
